Global Village privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Global Village is a student community app. If you sign in with Google, Google provides your account identifier, email address, name and, if available, profile photo. We use these details to create or find your Global Village account and show your identity in the app. We do not ask Google for access to your contacts, mail or files.

Information you choose to enter, including profile details, friendships, community activities, group and direct messages, and family information, is stored on the application's Azure server so it can be used across devices. If you grant location access, the community feature sends your location to that server to find nearby people and activities. The nearby-person location record is temporary; the app can update it while in use.

If you use the AI chat assistant, your message and recent AI conversation context are sent to Zhipu BigModel to generate a reply. AI chat history is also stored with your account on the application's server. Please do not enter confidential information into AI chat.

We use account information to provide sign-in and app features. We do not sell Google account information. The app communicates with Google for sign-in, Zhipu BigModel for AI requests, and Azure infrastructure to host its services. Access to private features requires an account. You may clear AI chat history in the app. For other data access or deletion requests, contact the app support address shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
